## Description

Pratala is a high-volatility, 16-bit pixel-art scatter-pays slot from Kilara, set in the hall of a
dragon too large to be seen whole: only its eye watches the board through a breach in the back
wall. The reels hold what its challengers left behind: a king's skull in a melted crown, a clawed
helm, a burnt wizard's hat, a bitten axe and four of their potions. Eight or more matching symbols
anywhere on the 6x5 grid pay, then are destroyed and the board tumbles.

Every cell holds a Glint Spot. The first win on a cell primes it, the second lights it at 2×, and
each one after that doubles it, up to 8×. A win is multiplied by the brightest lit spot beneath it.
Spots reset every base round, and on one spin in ten a Glint Strike lights three to six spots.

Four, five or six scatters award 10, 12 or 15 free spins, and three or more in the feature add five.
The back wall breaks open and the hall catches fire. Spots stay lit all feature, and every scatter
that lands is a Dragon's Gaze: fire from its eye runs down its reel and all five spots there double,
up to 4,096×, so a reel at 8× becomes 16×, then 32×. Six scatters start every spot at 128×.

Ante costs 3× the bet and makes free spins seven times as frequent. Boost costs 1.85× for a Glint
Strike on every spin. Bonus, Super and Hoard buy free spins for 100×, 205× and 435×: Super starts
every spot at 8×, Hoard at 4× with a Dragon's Gaze on one reel before every spin. RTP is 96.70% in
every mode, and the max win is 25,000× the bet.
