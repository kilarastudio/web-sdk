## Description

Pratala is a high-volatility, pixel-art scatter-pays slot from Kilara, set in a dragon's hall.
Eight or more matching symbols anywhere on the 6x5 grid pay, then explode and tumble.

Every cell holds a Glint Spot. The first explosion on a cell primes it, the second lights it at 2×,
and each one after that doubles it, up to 4,096×. A win is multiplied by the brightest lit spot
beneath it. Spots reset every base round, and on one spin in ten the dragon's Glint Strike lights
three to six spots at 2×, 4× or 8×.

Four, five or six scatters award 10, 12 or 15 free spins, and three or more during the feature add
five. Each session opens with a Glint Strike, and the spots stay lit until it ends.

Ante costs 3× the bet and makes free spins about seven times as frequent. Boost costs 1.9× and adds
a Glint Strike to every spin. The Bonus, Super and Hoard buys cost 100×, 350× and 640×,
and Super and Hoard start every spot at 4× and 8×. RTP is 96.70% in every mode, and the max win
is 25,000× the bet.
